### Changed

- **Ask Stackurity answers lead with a short recommendation.** The assistant now starts with the answer in a few sentences, then adds only the sections that apply: why, the evidence from your data, alternatives and their trade-offs, and data gaps (what is missing or not confirmed). It never says it did something: it recommends, and says where in Stackurity to make the change. An answer that lists what it could not check under data gaps is no longer retried on a stronger model for that alone. See [AI assistants](docs/ai-assistants.md#ask-stackurity).
