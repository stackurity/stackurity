### Changed

- **Ask Stackurity answers are easier to act on.** An answer written in the new layout leads with a short recommendation, with the reasoning, evidence, alternatives and data gaps in sections that open on a click (data gaps start open). Advisory ids (CVE-..., GHSA-...) and the issues and assets an answer names are links. Under each answer, **What Ask looked at** lists the lookups it made, linked to the issues and assets it read, and a line says it changed nothing: Ask can only read. Older answers look as before. See [AI assistants](docs/ai-assistants.md#ask-stackurity).
