### Changed

- **The Dashboard's setup checklist says why each step matters.** It now shows how far setup has got ("3 of 8 done", with a bar) and covers connecting a server, adding source repositories, turning on vulnerability alerts, setting who owns what and setting how issues are prioritised and fixed, besides inviting your team, connecting an AI assistant and two-factor sign-in. Each step says what doing it gets you, has a button to the page that does it, and is ticked from what exists in the workspace. On Community, automatic fixes show as part of Stackurity Pro. Finishing the first-run flow no longer hides the checklist, and it no longer disappears by itself: an administrator dismisses it once it is complete, or hides it earlier, for the whole workspace. Help, Your first ten minutes, lists the steps.
