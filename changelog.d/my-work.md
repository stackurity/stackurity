### Added

- **Fixes ▸ My work: one place to start the day.** A new page at `/fixes/mine` (the **My work** button on Fixes, and in its Views menu) lists, each as a short list with a count and one action per row: open issues assigned to you and your teams, what is overdue, requests to ignore waiting for your decision, automatic fixes waiting for approval or that failed (Pro), checks that did not clear their fix, and open incidents. Empty lists collapse to one line, such as "Nothing overdue". Filter by who it is assigned to (me and my teams, only me, anyone), project, priority and deadline. Approvals appear only for people who can decide them.
- **"Me and my teams" for assigned work.** The Fixes **Assigned to** filter, `GET /findings` and `GET /fix-groups` take `assignee=mine`: assigned to you or to a team you are in (`GET /findings` also takes `assignee=me`). `GET /checks?failed=true` lists the latest check of each fix from the last 30 days that did not clear it. See [the API](docs/api.md).
