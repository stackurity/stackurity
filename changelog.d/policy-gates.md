### Added

- **Policy gates (first part: rules, scopes and violations).** An organisation can write rules once and see, for every asset, what breaks them: no known exploited vulnerability, no component past its end of life, no open vulnerability of a given severity older than a number of days, and no open leaked key. Each rule can be narrowed to asset kinds, environments (`production` means the risk policy's production environments), internet-facing assets and tags, and is set to off, warn or enforce (new rules start in warn). Rules are scoped like notifications: the organisation's apply everywhere, and a project or asset can turn one off for itself, replace it with an edited copy, or add its own. Violations are worked out from current findings and leaked keys whenever they are read, and counted once an hour into a daily history. This release adds the API (`/api/v1/gate-policies`, `/api/v1/gate-policies/violations`, `/api/v1/projects/{id}/gate-policies`, `/api/v1/assets/{id}/gate-policies`) and the CI and deploy checks below; the dashboard pages follow. Every change is recorded in the audit log. See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md).
- **Policy gates in CI and at deploy.** `stackurity scan` now checks the organisation's policy gates once the server has evaluated the scan, when a policy applies to the repository: it prints a "Policy gates" block with each violation's policy, mode, reason and link. Violations of policies in warn mode pass; a violation of an enforced policy exits with status 3, as `--fail-on` does, and `--fail-on` flags are applied as well. `--policy=off` skips the check, and servers without policy gates are skipped without a word. `stackurity policy check --asset <id> | --image <digest> | --repo <url> [--json]` asks the same for deploy pipelines. Both use the new `POST /api/v1/gate-policies/check`, which honours approved exceptions and records every check (who, asset, commit, branch or image, result and violations). In the Community edition a policy in enforce mode counts as a warning and is marked "not enforced on this plan". See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md#in-ci).
