### Added

- **Policy gates (first part: rules, scopes and violations).** An organisation can write rules once and see, for every asset, what breaks them: no known exploited vulnerability, no component past its end of life, no open vulnerability of a given severity older than a number of days, and no open leaked key. Each rule can be narrowed to asset kinds, environments (`production` means the risk policy's production environments), internet-facing assets and tags, and is set to off, warn or enforce (new rules start in warn). Rules are scoped like notifications: the organisation's apply everywhere, and a project or asset can turn one off for itself, replace it with an edited copy, or add its own. Violations are worked out from current findings and leaked keys whenever they are read, and counted once an hour into a daily history. This release adds the API (`/api/v1/gate-policies`, `/api/v1/gate-policies/violations`, `/api/v1/projects/{id}/gate-policies`, `/api/v1/assets/{id}/gate-policies`) and the CI and deploy checks and dashboard pages below. Every change is recorded in the audit log. See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md).
- **Policy gates in CI and at deploy.** `stackurity scan` now checks the organisation's policy gates once the server has evaluated the scan, when a policy applies to the repository: it prints a "Policy gates" block with each violation's policy, mode, reason and link. Violations of policies in warn mode pass; a violation of an enforced policy exits with status 3, as `--fail-on` does, and `--fail-on` flags are applied as well. `--policy=off` skips the check, and servers without policy gates are skipped without a word. `stackurity policy check --asset <id> | --image <digest> | --repo <url> [--json]` asks the same for deploy pipelines. Both use the new `POST /api/v1/gate-policies/check`, which honours approved exceptions and records every check (who, asset, commit, branch or image, result and violations). In the Community edition a policy in enforce mode counts as a warning and is marked "not enforced on this plan". See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md#in-ci).
- **Policy gates in the dashboard and the weekly summary.** Settings → Policy gates lists the organisation's policies with their rule, mode, where each is turned off and how many violations it has, and adds or edits them (new policies start in warn; on a plan without enforce, Enforce shows a Pro badge and notice and checks report it as a warning). A project's and an asset's page have a Policy gates card to turn inherited policies off or on, edit them for that project or asset, or add their own. Fixes → Policy violations lists every violation with its policy, mode, asset, component, reason and link, filtered by policy, mode or rule. The Dashboard's Needs your attention band shows how many violations there are, and the weekly summary has a Policy gates section with each policy's open violations on the rule's assets. See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md#in-the-dashboard).
- **Policy gate exceptions.** When a violation cannot be fixed yet, anyone who can see the asset can ask for an exception: from a row of Fixes → Policy violations (this issue or leaked key, or every violation of the policy on the asset) or from a policy on an asset's Policy gates card, with a reason, a note and an end date within the risk policy's limit for exceptions. A security or admin user of the asset approves (optionally with an earlier end), declines (with a reason) or later ends it early on the new Approvals → Policy exceptions tab. While an exception is in force, what it covers is left out of the violations list, the Dashboard, the weekly summary and CI and deploy checks; on its end date it ends by itself and the violation counts again. The Violations page lists exceptions in force, and policies count them. Every step is audited (`policy_exception.request`, `.approve`, `.decline`, `.end`). New API: `/api/v1/gate-policies/exceptions` (list, ask), `/api/v1/gate-policies/exceptions/{id}`, `.../{id}/decision` and `.../{id}/end`. One approver decides, as for requests to ignore issues. See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md#exceptions).
- **Policy gates: enforce mode and the deploy gate on paid plans.** Two licence features, `gate_enforce` and `gate_deploy`, are in Pro and Enterprise (and Stackurity Cloud's Team and Business plans, trials included). With them an enforced policy fails CI jobs and deploy checks; without them it warns, as before. A deploy check (`stackurity policy check`, or `source: deploy`) without the deploy gate is still answered and recorded but never fails: the answer has `deploy_requires_plan: true` and the command prints a one-line notice, so deploy pipelines do not break when a plan changes. CI checks in warn mode stay free. Settings → Policy gates has a Deploy checks card with the command, and a Pro badge and notice on plans without it. See [Policy gates](docs/policy-gates.md#at-deploy).

### Upgrading

- Migration `0110_gate_policies` adds the tables `gate_policies`, `gate_policy_exclusions`, `gate_policy_exceptions`, `gate_policy_checks`, `gate_policy_open` and `gate_policy_daily`. It only adds tables; nothing existing changes.
