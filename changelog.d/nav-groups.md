### Changed

- **The sidebar is grouped the way the work is.** Under the Dashboard come **Security** (Fixes, All issues, Leaked keys, Automatic fixes, Incidents, Checks), **Environment** (Assets, Components, Projects, Coverage), **Governance** (Approvals, Reports and audit) and **Administration** (Integrations, People, Settings and, for instance administrators, Admin). Each group opens and closes and remembers that in your browser; the group of the page you are on opens by itself, and a closed group shows the counts waiting inside it. The counts moved to the items they are about: automatic fixes waiting on **Automatic fixes**, open SOS incidents on **Incidents**, requests to ignore on **Approvals**. Every address stays the same, items you cannot open are not shown, and the phone's **More** sheet lists the other pages by group. Breadcrumbs on detail pages now start with their group ("Security / Fixes / All issues / …").
- The documentation now names Admin (not Settings) for workspaces, the licence, platform health, coverage gaps, stack packs and What's new, and Integrations for API tokens.
