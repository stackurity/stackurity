### Added

- **Outcomes has an executive summary for managers, CTOs and CISOs.** Reports ▸ Outcomes now opens on a short **Executive summary**: open P1 and P2 issues, known exploited ones, issues past their fix-by date and assets reporting; business impact in plain words ("3 internet-facing production assets have an urgent or high-priority issue", shown only where environment and exposure are recorded); fixes made by their fix-by date; issues found against issues fixed per week; the ten most overdue issues with owner and age; open and overdue issues per person or team; and requests to ignore waiting for approval and exceptions that ran out. **Detailed** keeps every table as before, and each viewer's choice is remembered in their browser. **Print** gives a clean one- or two-page copy. Both views count exactly as the Dashboard and the evidence report do, and both explain how. See [the API reference](docs/api.md#remediation-outcomes).
- `GET /api/v1/reports/outcomes` returns `executive`: `trend` (`opened` and `fixed` per period), `overdue_top`, `by_owner`, `impact` and `exceptions`. No migration.
