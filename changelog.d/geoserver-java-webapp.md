### Added

- **GeoServer is identified and matched against NVD.** The agent reads GeoServer's release from `META-INF/maven/org.geoserver/gs-main/pom.properties` in its `WEB-INF/lib/gs-main-<release>.jar`, deployed in Tomcat or Jetty, unpacked or as a `.war` file, on servers and in images, and also in the `webapps` directory of GeoServer's binary distribution (`/usr/share/geoserver*`, `/opt/geoserver*`). It is reported as `pkg:generic/geoserver` and matched against NVD's records for `geoserver:geoserver` and `osgeo:geoserver`, which include the actively exploited CVE-2024-36401 and CVE-2025-58360, without needing the Java stack pack. The GeoServer flaws CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on 27 November 2026 (CVE-2026-120705, CVE-2026-129243; fixed in 2.27.7, 2.28.6 and 3.0.2) are matched once NVD records their affected versions, and every GeoServer row says so until then. See [Java web applications](docs/compatibility.md#java-web-applications).
